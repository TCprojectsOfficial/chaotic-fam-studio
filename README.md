CHAOTIC FAM STUDIO

A connected marketplace for independent music creators

Built under "TCprojects" (https://tcprojects.lovable.app/portfolio)

CHAOTIC FAM STUDIO is a music creator platform designed to connect independent artists, producers and creative professionals through a shared digital environment for music discovery, creator profiles, digital products and creative services.

The project is focused on supporting the independent music creator community with tools and marketplace experiences that bring creative work and opportunities closer together.

«Project status: In development. Core platform modules have been reported as built, and the manual EFT checkout foundation has completed its first three development phases. Further payment workflow development and final verification remain outstanding.»

---

Project Overview

CHAOTIC FAM STUDIO began as a browser-based digital audio workstation concept and evolved into a broader creator marketplace and digital studio ecosystem.

The platform's direction is to provide independent creators with a central place to present their work, discover other creators, explore music products and access creative services.

The project is being developed by TCprojects, a digital platforms and systems studio focused on building technology-driven products and business platforms.

The Problem

Independent music creators often rely on multiple disconnected services and communication channels to showcase their work, discover collaborators, access creative services and sell digital products.

CHAOTIC FAM STUDIO aims to bring these activities together in one connected digital environment.

The Vision

To build an accessible creator ecosystem that supports independent music culture, creative collaboration and digital commerce.

Target Audience

- Independent recording artists
- Music producers and beatmakers
- Songwriters and recording creators
- Music-related service providers
- Independent creative professionals

Industry

Music Technology · Creator Economy · Digital Marketplaces · Creative Platforms

Administration

- Administrative Control Room
- Recording moderation workflows
- Marketplace administration
- Banking settings restricted to authorised Super Admins

Manual EFT Commerce

The first three phases of the manual EFT checkout roadmap have been completed.

- Restricted administrative banking settings
- Server-controlled checkout configuration
- Server-side product price retrieval
- Pending order creation and reuse
- Unique EFT payment references
- Duplicate pending-order prevention
- Existing customer and purchased-file access restrictions preserved

Important: EFT checkout is currently disabled. Proof-of-payment submission, administrative payment verification, and the remaining fulfilment workflow are not yet confirmed as complete.
